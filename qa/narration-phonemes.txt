Cognitive reappraisal: changing an interpretation to alter an emotional response. Expressive suppression: inhibiting outward emotional expression. Regulatory flexibility: adjusting strategies to goals and conditions.

Reappraisal may change the meaning of an ambiguous event. Suppression changes visible expression without necessarily resolving the feeling or situation.

Neither strategy is universally good or bad. Safety, culture, goals and timing affect usefulness. Forced positivity can obscure a real problem that needs action.
